Lesson 3 · 15 min

Ask the agent: the RISE method and the five-frame test

A vague request produces generic animation and ten rounds of fixes. RISE organizes the request into four parts: References, Idea, Style, and Examine. The last one saves you the most: the agent checks five frames and fixes things before you render. In this lesson you write the request and check the result with a command.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1The four parts of the request and what goes in each
  2. 2The snapshot: five frames in seconds, without rendering the video
  3. 3The loop seam checked by pixel
  4. 4The 10 failures of an amateur animation, to look for in the frames

Goal: write a RISE request for your agent and check the result in five frames before rendering.

02 · The finished request

A 6 s loop over an editing timeline. Copy it into the project folder and paste it into the agent (Claude Code or Codex, with the top model of your subscription).

File · kit/prompts/rise-loop.md
R — REFERENCES
Read the DESIGN.md in this folder before anything else: colors, fonts and motion rules come from it. Do not invent a logo, color or font.
HyperFrames 0.8.176 project in this folder (index.html already created by init).

I — IDEA
A 6-second loop, 1920x1080, over an editing timeline.
- Start (0–0.6 s): the timeline track and the playhead stopped on the left.
- Middle (0.6–4.2 s): the playhead runs to the right; the title "Cena 1" enters and leaves.
- End (4.2–6 s): the playhead goes back to the left and stops. The last frame is the same as the first.

S — STYLE
Look: background #0c0c10 with soft light in the center, track #3f3f47, playhead #e9b949 (the only accent color), white title #f4f4f5 in the system sans-serif, weight 700.
Motion: nothing at constant speed (power1 to power3, soft ease-in and ease-out); the title stays legible for at least 1.2 s; one thing moves at a time.

E — EXAMINE
Write everything in a single paused GSAP timeline registered in window.__timelines["main"], with no Math.random and no Date.now.
Run `npx hyperframes@0.8.176 check` and fix until "Check passed" shows up.
Take 5 frames with `npx hyperframes@0.8.176 snapshot --at 0,1.5,3,4.5,5.9 --no-end -o quadros`, look at the contact-sheet.jpg and check:
the 0 s frame is the same as the 5.9 s frame; no cut-off letters; no single word alone on a line; the title appears in full in the 3 s frame.
Fix whatever fails and repeat until everything passes. Do not render the MP4: I render it after I see the frames.
In the agent · inside the project folder
Read rise-loop.md and do exactly what it asks. At the end, tell me the check result and what you fixed after looking at the 5 frames.

03 · The E in your hands: five frames and the seam

The kit/exemplos/a3-loop folder has a composition made from this request. The rehearsal runs the check, takes five frames, and compares the first with the last.

The frames are saved in quadros/ (frame-00-at-0s.png through frame-04-at-5.9s.png) with a contact-sheet.jpg, the grid that you and the agent look at.

5 quadros em quadros/
COSTURA: diferente — o último quadro não volta ao primeiro
saída: 1

Without the last movement, the playhead ends on the right and the loop jumps. The command catches the failure before any render.

In 0.8.176, snapshot has the --describe option, which sends the frames to Gemini for analysis and runs on its own when GEMINI_API_KEY exists in the environment (text from snapshot --help). The blocks run unset GEMINI_API_KEY so nothing leaves the machine.

04 · What to look for in the five frames

The 10 motion-rise failures, in four groups. Paste the list at the end of the request or check it yourself in the contact-sheet.

1. Text

Clipped letters (line height 1.1 or more; check g, j, p, q, y).

A single word alone on the last line: rewrite it, or tie the last two words together with a non-breaking space.

Every piece of text holds still for at least 1.2 s.

2. Stage

The visual fills 80% to 95% of the frame.

Light falls off across the frame: no flat color.

One idea on a calm background.

3. Motion

Nothing at constant speed: smooth entrances and exits.

In a loop, the last frame matches the first (costura.sh checks it).

4. Brand

Logo only from the file, never drawn from memory.

Subtle texture so it doesn't look like plastic.
